# FeDHLab Project Registry

The **FeDHLab Project Registry** provides a structured catalogue of research projects, software, datasets and digital resources developed within or in collaboration with the **Federico II Digital Humanities Laboratory (FeDHLab)**.

FeDHLab is based at the Department of Humanities, University of Naples Federico II.

## Purpose

The registry is intended to:

- provide a common entry point to FeDHLab digital research activities;
- document projects, software, datasets and technical resources;
- connect GitHub repositories with institutional websites and research infrastructures;
- improve discoverability, reuse and citation of digital research outputs;
- support long-term documentation and preservation;
- promote open, reproducible and FAIR research practices.

## Registry structure

The registry is organised into four main sections:

- `projects/` — research projects and infrastructures;
- `software/` — research software, tools and computational workflows;
- `datasets/` — datasets and structured research data;
- `templates/` — standard templates for documenting FeDHLab resources.

## Projects

Project records will progressively be added to the `projects/` directory.

Each record may include:

- project title and acronym;
- responsible researcher / Principal Investigator;
- participating institutions and research units;
- funding programme;
- project duration and status;
- project website;
- related GitHub repositories;
- software and datasets;
- standards and technologies;
- licenses;
- DOI and persistent identifiers;
- related publications.

## Software and datasets

Software and datasets may have dedicated GitHub repositories.

Large files, image collections, preservation masters and other research objects unsuitable for GitHub should be deposited in appropriate institutional or disciplinary repositories. Their metadata and persistent links can nevertheless be registered here.

## Contributing

New records should follow the templates available in the `templates/` directory.

The registry is maintained by FeDHLab.
