## Description

The Naples Dante Project (NDP) is a digital research environment for the study of Dante's textual and material tradition.

It integrates textual, codicological, iconographic and bibliographical resources and provides a common infrastructure for the description, digitisation, analysis and computational processing of Dante manuscripts, fragments, early printed editions and related visual materials.

NDP develops and connects a series of research initiatives devoted to the *Commedia*, including manuscript census and digitisation, textual criticism, iconography, fragment studies, critical editions and computational philology.
